# Document → Executive Summary

**Paste a long report, contract or policy, get a one-page plain-English brief back.**

For the 40-page document you need to understand before a meeting, sign off on, or explain to someone else.

## What you get

- **Bottom line:** the one thing you need to know
- The key points, most important first, with the real numbers and dates
- Risks and watch-outs: anything costly, unusual, one-sided or missing
- What you need to do, and by when
- A quick table of key dates and figures

## Try it

> Give me an executive summary of this:
>
> *[paste the document, or attach it]*

Other ways to ask:

- "I have to approve this contract tomorrow. What do I need to know?"
- "Summarise this policy for the leadership team. What changes for staff?"
- "I'm meeting this vendor on Friday. Brief me on their proposal and what to push back on."
- "Make it half as long."

No document handy? Use [`examples/sample-document.md`](examples/sample-document.md).

## Tips

- Tell it **why** you're reading. "I need to decide whether to renew" gets a much sharper brief than "summarise this".
- Very long documents may exceed what your AI tool can read in one go. If that happens, summarise it section by section, then ask for a combined brief.
- For contracts and anything high-stakes, use the summary to know where to look, then read those sections yourself or with a specialist.
