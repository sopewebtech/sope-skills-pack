**MANAGED PRINT SERVICES AGREEMENT**
Between Brightline Office Solutions Pty Ltd ("Provider") and the Customer

**1. Term.** This Agreement commences on the Commencement Date and continues for an Initial Term of thirty-six (36) months. Thereafter, the Agreement shall automatically renew for successive terms of twelve (12) months unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal no less than ninety (90) days prior to the expiry of the then-current term.

**2. Fees.** The Customer shall pay a Monthly Service Fee of $1,850 (excluding GST), which includes up to 20,000 mono pages and 4,000 colour pages per month across all Devices. Pages in excess of the included volume shall be charged at $0.012 per mono page and $0.09 per colour page. The Provider may adjust the Monthly Service Fee annually on each anniversary of the Commencement Date by the greater of CPI or 5%.

**3. Devices.** The Provider will supply six (6) multifunction Devices. Title in the Devices remains with the Provider at all times. The Customer is responsible for loss of or damage to Devices while at the Customer's premises, other than fair wear and tear.

**4. Service levels.** The Provider will use reasonable endeavours to respond to fault reports within eight (8) business hours. Consumables (excluding paper) are included.

**5. Termination.** The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time on ninety (90) days' written notice, subject to payment of an Early Termination Fee equal to 80% of the Monthly Service Fees payable for the remainder of the Initial Term.

**6. Liability.** The Provider's total aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid by the Customer in the three (3) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. The Provider is not liable for any loss of data stored on Devices.

**7. Data.** At the end of the Term, the Provider will collect the Devices. Hard-drive data wiping is available on request at an additional charge of $150 per Device.
