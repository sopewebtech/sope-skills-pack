# Inbox Triage & Reply Drafts

**Paste in a messy email thread, get two or three reply options at different tones.**

For the long reply-all chain you've been avoiding, or the email where you know *what* to say but not *how*.

## What you get

- **Where this is at:** the thread in two or three sentences
- What they're actually asking you, the deadline, and how urgent it is
- Two or three ready-to-send replies, for example **Direct**, **Warm** and **Firm**, all saying the same thing in a different tone
- A "before you send" checklist of placeholders and things to confirm
- A clear warning if the email looks like a scam or phishing attempt

## Try it

> Help me reply to this:
>
> *[paste the email thread]*

Other ways to ask:

- "I want to say no to this without burning the relationship."
- "I need another week. Give me a holding reply."
- "Catch me up on this thread. I've been on leave."
- "Give me one option that says yes and one that says no."

No thread handy? Use [`examples/sample-thread.md`](examples/sample-thread.md).

## Tips

- Paste the **whole** thread, including older messages. Context changes the right answer.
- Tell it what you want to say, if you know. It's much better at *how* than at guessing *what*.
- Using the **Voice & Tone Matcher** in the same chat? Paste your voice profile first and every option will sound like you.
- Nothing is connected to your mailbox. You copy the thread in, you copy your chosen reply out.
