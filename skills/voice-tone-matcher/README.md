# Voice & Tone Matcher

**Paste in a few of your own emails once, then get new drafts written in your voice.**

Most AI drafts sound like AI. This skill studies how *you* write (your greetings, sign-offs, length, habits and the phrases you reuse) and writes new messages to match.

## What you get

- A **voice profile**: a short description of how you write, which you can save and reuse
- Drafts of emails, replies, Teams messages or LinkedIn posts that sound like you
- A note wherever it had to guess, and placeholders instead of made-up facts

## Try it

**First time:**

> I want you to learn how I write. Here are four emails I've sent recently:
>
> *[paste 3 to 5 of your own emails]*

**Then:**

> Write a reply to this saying I can't make Thursday but can do Monday morning:
>
> *[paste the email you're replying to]*

**Next time:** paste your saved voice profile first, then ask for the draft. There's no need to re-share your samples.

No samples handy? Use [`examples/sample-emails.md`](examples/sample-emails.md) to see how it works.

## Tips

- Use emails **you** wrote. Remove any quoted replies from other people.
- A mix of formal and casual samples gives the best range.
- If you edit a draft, paste your edited version back. The skill will update your profile.
- "Make it more formal" keeps your voice and just dials it up. It won't turn you into someone else.

## Privacy

Your samples stay in your chat. Nothing is connected to your mailbox, and you choose exactly what to paste. Only share what you're comfortable having in your AI tool, and follow your organisation's AI use policy.
