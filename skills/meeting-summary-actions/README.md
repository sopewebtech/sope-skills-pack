# Meeting Transcript → Summary & Actions

**Paste a transcript, get a clean summary with decisions, owners and action items.**

Works on any transcript from anywhere: Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, Otter, a phone recording, or your own typed notes. You don't need calendar or Teams access.

## What you get

- A 3 to 5 sentence summary of what happened and where things landed
- The decisions that were made (the final position, not the back-and-forth)
- An action table with owner and due date, and "unassigned" where nobody put their hand up
- Open questions, plus anything that looks mis-transcribed and is worth checking

## Try it

> Summarise this meeting and pull out the actions:
>
> *[paste transcript]*

Other ways to ask:

- "Write this up as minutes I can send to the attendees."
- "My manager wasn't in this meeting. Give them the two-minute version."
- "Just the actions, please, grouped by owner."
- "Now draft a follow-up email to everyone with the actions."

No transcript handy? Use [`examples/sample-transcript.md`](examples/sample-transcript.md).

## Tips

- **Getting a transcript out of Teams:** open the meeting recap, go to *Transcript*, then *Download*. Zoom and Google Meet have similar options if transcription was turned on.
- Speaker labels make owners far more accurate. If your transcript doesn't have them, the skill won't guess who said what.
- The skill only includes what was said. If an owner or date is missing, that's usually a sign it wasn't actually agreed in the meeting.
