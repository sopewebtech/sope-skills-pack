## The five skills

| Skill | Paste in… | Get back… |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| [**Meeting Transcript → Summary & Actions**](skills/meeting-summary-actions/) | A meeting transcript or rough notes | A clean summary with decisions, owners and action items |
| [**Voice & Tone Matcher**](skills/voice-tone-matcher/) | A few of your own past emails (once) | New drafts written in your voice |
| [**Document → Executive Summary**](skills/executive-summary/) | A long report, contract or policy | A one-page plain-English brief |
| [**Inbox Triage & Reply Drafts**](skills/inbox-triage-replies/) | A messy email thread | What's being asked, plus 2 to 3 reply options at different tones |
| [**Plain-English Rewriter**](skills/plain-english-rewriter/) | Dense jargon, *or* rough notes | Plain English, *or* a properly structured document |

Each skill folder has a short guide, example prompts and a fictional sample so you can try it before using your own content.

### No-setup option: copy and paste

Every skill is one file called `SKILL.md`. To use a skill in any AI chat tool:

1. Open the skill's `SKILL.md` (for example [this one](skills/meeting-summary-actions/SKILL.md)) and click the **Copy raw file** button.
2. Paste it into a new chat, then add your content underneath: the transcript, document or email thread.
3. Send.

It takes an extra step each time, but it works with any AI tool.

## Using these safely

- **Follow your organisation's AI policy.** Only paste content you're allowed to share with your AI tool. If you're not sure which tools are approved for work content, ask your IT team.
- **Check the output.** The skills are written to stick to what you give them, flag gaps, and never invent names, dates or numbers. But you're still the editor. Read before you send.
- **Not professional advice.** The Executive Summary and Plain-English skills help you understand contracts and policies. For anything legal, financial or high-stakes, they'll point out what to take to a specialist.

## FAQ

**Do I need a paid AI plan?**
Copy and paste works on any plan. Saving a skill permanently (as a Copilot agent, Claude skill or custom GPT) may need a paid or work plan, depending on the tool. See the install guides.

**Does this connect to my email, calendar or files?**
No. Nothing is connected. You paste content in and copy the result out.

**Can I change the skills?**
Yes. They're plain text. Edit the `SKILL.md` to suit your team: add your house style, your meeting template, your sign-off.

**Can I share this with colleagues?**
Please do. See [LICENSE](LICENSE).

**Will there be more skills?**
Yes. Watch or star this repo to hear about new releases.
